This is a regional on-site rental, not Russellville delivery.

This is an on-site regional option, not a Russellville rental shop or a confirmed delivery service.     Location: 920 Gossage Circle, Plainview, AR 72857 · Phone: 800-817-6148 · Source: operator-authored cabin listing and official park website.

    Host's bicycle-rental description ↗ Official campground contact ↗

    Campus-only · Free for enrolled students

    Arkansas Tech University Outdoor Recreation

    Arkansas Tech University officially offers free bicycle checkouts for currently enrolled students, alongside kayaks, canoes, tents, hammocks and other outdoor gear. Its official Campus Recreation resources also allow faculty and staff with an employee recreation membership to use Outdoor Recreation equipment checkouts; membership terms and any staff checkout charges require confirmation. This is a restricted campus lending program, not a public bike-rental shop. A valid university identification card and applicable waivers or safety orientations are required. Return equipment on time; late, lost or damaged items may incur charges. Confirm eligibility, available bicycles and checkout periods with campus staff.

    Location: 215 West O Street, Russellville, Arkansas 72801 · Regular hours: Monday–Friday, 10 a.m.–6 p.m.; summer hours differ. Equipment availability unconfirmed.
