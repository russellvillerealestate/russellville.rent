Student-only · No-cost equipment checkout

Arkansas Tech University Outdoor Recreation

Arkansas Tech University officially offers free bicycle checkouts for currently enrolled students, alongside kayaks, canoes, tents, hammocks and other outdoor gear. This is a university equipment-lending program, not a public bike-rental shop. A valid Arkansas Tech student identification card is required; students must complete applicable waivers and safety orientations, return equipment on time, and may be charged for late, lost or damaged items. Confirm available bicycles and checkout periods with campus staff.

Location: 215 West O Street, Russellville, Arkansas 72801 · Regular hours: Monday–Friday, 10 a.m.–6 p.m.; summer hours differ. Equipment availability unconfirmed.
