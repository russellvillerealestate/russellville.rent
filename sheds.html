BEFORE THE BUILDING ARRIVES / SITE PLANNING

Think beyond the footprint.

A shed that fits on paper still needs an accessible delivery route, a permitted placement location and clear setup terms. Keep these questions handy when calling a dealer.

01Can the delivery crew reach the location?+

Ask about gate width, driveway turns, grade, ground conditions, trees and overhead obstructions. Request the dealer's required clearances for its delivery equipment rather than relying on an example diagram.

02Is the building placement permitted?+

Verify property boundaries, easements, utility routes, permits, setbacks and community restrictions with the appropriate authorities. Ask the dealer what type of prepared site or base is required.

03What is included on delivery day?+

Get written terms for delivery distance, any charges, leveling, supports, anchoring, setup, scheduling and fees if the crew cannot access the planned location.

Before you commit

Four questions worth asking.

Rent-to-own agreements can differ. Confirm the details directly before arranging a building or delivery.

01 · TOTAL COSTWhat will I pay in total if I complete the agreement?
02 · DELIVERYIs delivery to my address available, and what does it cost?
03 · OWNERSHIPWhen does ownership transfer, and what happens if I stop paying?
04 · SITE READINESSWhat access, foundation, and site preparation are required?

Take these questions to the dealers. Compare the complete written agreement and confirm site access before arranging delivery.
