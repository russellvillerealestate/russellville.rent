      Four checks / Open for details

      Before a driver heads your way.

      Use this field guide to describe your site accurately. The rental company must determine whether delivery and placement are possible.

      01How does the truck get in?

      Describe the entry road, driveway, gate, slopes and turning area. Ask the operator to verify vehicle access rather than assuming a truck can reach the spot.

      02Where could the container sit?

      Identify the preferred surface and whether it is on private property or a public street. Ask about site protection, necessary permission, placement restrictions and pickup access.

      03What's around or overhead?

      Mention trees, overhead wires, roofs, fences, parked vehicles and other obstructions. Do not estimate safe clearances yourself; have the operator confirm requirements.

      04What will go inside?

      Explain the debris mix, approximate project scope and expected rental dates. Confirm prohibited items, included weight, fill limits and extra pickup or disposal charges.
