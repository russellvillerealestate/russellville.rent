A clearer comparison

Four questions before you sign.

A lower periodic payment does not always mean a lower total cost. Ask for the complete agreement and compare like for like.

01 / TOTAL COST

What will I pay in all?

Ask about the total of payments, fees and any taxes.

02 / OWNERSHIP

When would I own it?

Clarify the ownership conditions and any early-purchase options.

03 / FLEXIBILITY

What if I need to return it?

Review return, cancellation and missed-payment terms.

04 / DELIVERY

What comes with the item?

Confirm delivery, setup, maintenance and condition.
