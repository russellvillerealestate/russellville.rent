The River Valley field guide / A better first estimate

Before the panels, picture the perimeter.

A clear sketch makes it easier to describe a site to a fencing supplier. Measure your own layout, then ask the operator to confirm panels, gates, delivery, installation and rental terms.

02 / What suppliers need to know

  1. 01

    Trace every fence run.

    Walk the edges you want fenced. Add the lengths in feet, including jogs and separate sections.

  2. 02

    Mark every opening.

    Show pedestrian and vehicle access points. Ask which gate widths and barrier types the supplier offers.

  3. 03

    Flag site conditions.

    Note slopes, pavement, soft ground, utilities and delivery access. Photos can help a supplier prepare a quote.
