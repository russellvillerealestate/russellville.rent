Comparison notes

Enter the base rental quote for one or both options to compare estimated costs.

How the worksheet adds costs: estimated spend = base quote + mileage/overage + other nonrefundable charges. “Including refundable deposit” adds a deposit for cash-flow planning but does not treat it as an expense. Blank extra fields are treated as zero; confirm them rather than assuming they are waived. Prices and totals are your own estimates, not provider quotes, reservations or amounts guaranteed due at pickup. Confirm one-way restrictions, vehicle size, rental duration, taxes, fuel, insurance, refund conditions and final pricing directly. This worksheet does not save or transmit entries.
