Cogswell Motors Rentals

Cars, trucks, minivans, sport utility vehicles, and 15-passenger vans. Official rental terms require drivers to be at least 21 with a valid driver's license and a major credit card. Standard included mileage is 200 miles/day, 1,400 miles/week or 3,000 miles/month unless otherwise specified. The operator advertises a 10% discount for corporate customers and nonprofit organizations (not combinable with other discounts) and after-hours service for an additional fee. Cogswell Mazda's official rental page also advertises rental-vehicle delivery to Russellville Regional Airport; confirm whether the service is available for your booking, plus any timing requirements or fees.
