Long Pool Recreation Area — Near Dover

U.S. Forest Service campground in the Ozark–St. Francis National Forests near Dover. Recreation.gov identifies non-electric campsites and first-come, first-served only camping: visitors must arrive in person to claim and pay for a site; advance reservations are not offered through Recreation.gov. Confirm seasonal access, fees and site conditions before traveling. This is one Forest Service campground, not a separate private rental business.

Cove Lake Recreation Area — Near Paris

U.S. Forest Service campground in the Ozark–St. Francis National Forests, about 15 miles south of Paris. Officially lists tent and RV campsites with tables and fire rings, flush toilets, hot showers and drinking water; no campsite hookups. Some sites may be first-come, first-served. Confirm current operating dates, site booking arrangements and fees before visiting. This is another site managed by the same Forest Service, not a separate private rental operator.

White Rock Mountain Recreation Area — Near Mulberry (Regional)

Ozark–St. Francis National Forests recreation area with nine non-hookup campsites, three historic stone cabins and a stone lodge available for overnight rental. The official federal booking page lists year-round lodging and campground access; some campsites accommodate small recreational vehicles or pop-up campers. This is a regional mountain destination, not a Russellville property. Confirm reservation availability, road access and current policies before traveling. The cabins and campground are one recreation-area operator, not separate rental businesses.
