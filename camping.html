Lake Dardanelle State Park — Russellville & Dardanelle Areas

One state-park operator with 57 campsites in the Russellville area (16 Class AAA, 14 Class AA and 27 Class B) and 18 Class B sites in the Dardanelle area. Spadra Campground — Clarksville

U.S. Army Corps of Engineers campground with 24 campsites, many offering electric and water hookups. Reservations and operating dates should be confirmed with Recreation.gov; no telephone number is verified on its official campground listing.

Petit Jean State Park — Morrilton

Arkansas State Parks operates year-round campsites, a group camping area, cabins and four rentable yurts at Petit Jean Mountain. This is a regional destination near Morrilton, not a Russellville campground. Confirm site type, rates and availability with the park.
