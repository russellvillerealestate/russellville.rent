Three questions for your first call.

Use these with any listed operator. Ask for written confirmation of the rental terms before driving to a pickup point.

  1. 1
    Can our group drive it?Minimum age, licenses, insurance, passengers, seating, safety gear.
  2. 2
    Where is it allowed?Permitted trails or park boundaries, pickup location and delivery limitations.
  3. 3
    What is the entire cost?Rental duration, deposits, insurance, fuel, fees and cancellation rules.

THE TRAIL CLEARANCE / THREE QUESTIONS

Ready for the road?

Before the pickup, get three clear answers directly from the rental operator. Routes, rates and driver rules can change.

Revisit the rental listings ↑

Ask where to ride.

Verify permitted roads and trails, current forest restrictions, and whether the rental must remain on private property. Do not assume a rental can leave its property.

Confirm costs and coverage.

Ask for total fees, damage deposits, insurance requirements, fuel terms, cancellation policy and any mileage or delivery charges.

Confirm driver eligibility.

Age and driver’s license rules vary by provider. Ask about passengers, vehicle seating and appropriate safety gear before booking.
