Ask where to ride.

Verify permitted roads and trails, current forest restrictions, and whether the rental must remain on private property. Do not assume a rental can leave its property.

Confirm costs and coverage.

Ask for total fees, damage deposits, insurance requirements, fuel terms, cancellation policy and any mileage or delivery charges.

Confirm driver eligibility.

Age and driver’s license rules vary by provider. Ask about passengers, vehicle seating and appropriate safety gear before booking.
