COOPER MUSIC / LOCAL STORE ♫ RIVER VALLEY SUNSET · EDITORIAL SCENE, NOT A STOREFRONT PHOTO

Cooper Music — Russellville

Local band instrument store advertising in-house rent-to-own for student and step-up instruments, including saxophones, trumpets and trombones. The official program advertises a first payment upfront plus 24 monthly payments at 11.29% interest. Returns are accepted, but confirm the specific instrument's return window (one published trombone offer says after four months), total cost and any fees before signing. Separate Klarna/Synchrony promotional purchase financing is not a rental. The operator's published school route schedules Russellville and Pottsville on Mondays and Dardanelle on Wednesdays; the operator states there are no Friday deliveries. The in-house rent-to-own application requires a bank account and first payment. The operator says applicants will receive an approval decision in one to three business days and that an initial payment is refunded if an application is denied; approved instruments are delivered to the school's band director. These are published policies and route days, not a guaranteed delivery appointment or proof of current stock. Confirm eligibility, instrument availability, total costs and school delivery directly.

Before you enroll

Four questions worth asking.

Instrument programs are not short-term event sound-equipment rentals. Confirm the agreement that fits your student and school.

01 / COST

What is the total cost?

Ask about monthly payments, fees, purchase credits and the full ownership amount.

02 / CARE

What repairs are covered?

Check maintenance, replacement and damage coverage before signing.

03 / LOGISTICS

How does delivery work?

Confirm pickup, school delivery routes and return arrangements for your address.

04 / SCHOOL FIT

Is it the right instrument?

Verify the required model, instrument selection and compatibility with the band program.
