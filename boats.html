Know before you go

Federal marina closure notices

Full closure · Russellville Marina

Important closure notice — October 23, 2026: The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers ordered a full closure of Russellville Marina, including its grounds, docks, cabins, store, repair shop and all commercial operations. Boat and cabin rentals at this marina are not available. Background: The Corps first closed unsafe docks on July 8, 2026, citing damage from a February winter storm compounded by deferred maintenance. The operator had not completed required safety measures and corrective repairs before the October 23 full closure. Official July dock-safety notice ↗ The operator website may still advertise rentals, but the federal closure supersedes that information. Do not attempt to access closed facilities.

Personal property access

Boat owners and marina patrons: The general property-removal window ended October 30, 2026. Removal from open marina areas is now by appointment only, coordinated through the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers. Previously closed docks must not be entered without explicit coordination. Property remaining after July 7, 2027 may be subject to federal impoundment and removal. For access instructions, contact the Russellville Site Office at 501-340-1762 or the Corps Real Estate Division at 501-324-5716 (weekdays, 8 a.m.–4 p.m.). Do not enter closed or unsafe facilities. October 23 closure report ↗ · Official Corps site-office contacts ↗

Separate closure · Dardanelle Marina

Separate Dardanelle Marina closure: The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers also closed Dardanelle Marina (Theriot Marina) on December 31, 2025 after its lease was not renewed because of public-safety and lease-compliance issues. It is not an active boat-rental option. Official Corps closure notice, January 8, 2026 ↗.
