RUSSELLVILLE, ARKANSAS & THE RIVER VALLEY

Boat, cabin & marina rentals

Important closure notice — October 23, 2026: The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers ordered a full closure of Russellville Marina, including its grounds, docks, cabins, store, repair shop and all commercial operations. Boat and cabin rentals at this marina are not available. The operator website may still advertise rentals, but the federal closure supersedes that information. Do not attempt to access closed facilities.

Boat owners and marina patrons: The Corps permits removal of personal property from nonhazardous lease areas through October 30, 2026. Previously closed docks must not be entered without coordination. After October 30, removal from open marina areas is by appointment only. For access instructions, contact the Russellville Site Office at 501-340-1762 or the Corps Real Estate Division at 501-324-5716 (weekdays, 8 a.m.–4 p.m.). Do not enter closed or unsafe facilities. Separate Dardanelle Marina closure: The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers also closed Dardanelle Marina (Theriot Marina) on December 31, 2025 after its lease was not renewed because of public-safety and lease-compliance issues.
