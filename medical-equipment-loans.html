Goodwill Industries of Arkansas — Health Equipment Loan Program (HELP)

Goodwill's statewide Health Equipment Loan Program provides gently used medical equipment free of charge to Arkansas residents, with no set return deadline. Items may include wheelchairs, walkers, crutches, canes, commodes and shower chairs, depending on donations. Call or visit the Russellville retail store to ask about stock; if unavailable locally, Goodwill says it can check statewide inventory and arrange transfer to the nearest Goodwill location. Specific equipment availability in Russellville is UNCONFIRMED.

02 / STATEWIDE PROGRAM↗

Increasing Capabilities Access Network (iCAN) — Arkansas statewide

Arkansas's statewide assistive-technology program offers device loans, demonstrations, reuse and exchange services. The official program states its services are available to Arkansans regardless of geographic area, age, disability or income. Device loans are distinct from cash loans or retail rentals. Confirm whether the specific device is loanable, any requirements, and pickup or return arrangements; Russellville delivery is UNCONFIRMED.

03 / ELIGIBILITY-RESTRICTED STATE PROGRAM＋

Arkansas Spinal Cord Commission — Regional Loan Closets

The Arkansas Spinal Cord Commission operates regional equipment loan closets for its eligible clients with spinal cord disabilities, not the general public. Case managers can arrange short-term loans for emergencies, while equipment is being repaired or purchased, or to try a suitable device. Potential items include manual or power wheelchairs, walkers, portable ramps, therapeutic cushions and adaptive bathroom equipment. The agency states that all of its clients may use the loan program regardless of income. Current inventory, a Russellville pickup location and delivery to Russellville are UNCONFIRMED. Contact the commission for eligibility and a case-manager referral before traveling.
