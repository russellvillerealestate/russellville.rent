Medical equipment loans & assistive technology

Free equipment lending and statewide assistive-technology device loans for Arkansas residents. These programs are not commercial equipment rentals. Ask about eligibility, available equipment, pickup, loan duration and safe fit before traveling.

Goodwill Industries of Arkansas — Health Equipment Loan Program (HELP)

Goodwill's statewide Health Equipment Loan Program provides gently used medical equipment free of charge to Arkansas residents, with no set return deadline. Items may include wheelchairs, walkers, crutches, canes, commodes and shower chairs, depending on donations. Call or visit the Russellville retail store to ask about stock; if unavailable locally, Goodwill says it can check statewide inventory and arrange transfer to the nearest Goodwill location. Increasing Capabilities Access Network (iCAN) — Arkansas statewide

Arkansas's statewide assistive-technology program offers device loans, demonstrations, reuse and exchange services. The official program states its services are available to Arkansans regardless of geographic area, age, disability or income. Device loans are distinct from cash loans or retail rentals. Confirm whether the specific device is loanable, any requirements, and pickup or return arrangements; Russellville delivery is UNCONFIRMED.
